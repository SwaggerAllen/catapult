# Chain-axis load-time rules, classified by runtime dependence

Method: every runtime reader of the `Chain`/`Tier`/`Edge` structs outside
`lib/catapult/dsl/` was read (`generation/{sweeper,dispatch_worker,
commit_path,context_assembly,extraction,runtime}.ex`, `engine/projections/
{ready_scopes,context_resolver,staleness,graph_constraints,
predicate_evaluator}.ex`, `engine/reducer.ex`, `engine/scheduler.ex`,
`delivery/provisioning.ex`, `catapult_web/live/explain_why_live.ex`) plus
`dsl/edge_locator.ex` (shared). Fields with **no** runtime reader at all:
`Chain.fragments`, `Chain.flows`, `Tier.delivery`, `Tier.enforcement`,
`Tier.extra`, `Tier.executor`, `Edge.navigation`, `Edge.consistency`,
`Edge.constraint_raw`, `Flow.*`, a review tier's own `context`,
`cardinality.when`/`per_source`, and a `:self` walk's `target_tier`
(`ContextResolver.resolve/2` ignores it; only `all.<tier>` uses it).

## Summary

Counts (37 rows): **ENGINE 22**, **LOADER-ONLY 17**, **HABIT 2**, **NEGATIVE 3** (rows 8, 56, and the unbuilt prompt/schema-path claim in 2e; the "no cross-axis check" half of 12 is also negative).

Of the ENGINE rows, the runtime needs a **weaker form** for: #4 (fanout instances only), #2b (scope_filter slot only), #3b→not needed, #5 (integer typing only), #64 (non-dispatchable reference tier only), `target_problem` (`all.<tier>` only). One rule is **weaker than the runtime**: `produces` owner — Extraction only honours `self.parent`. The ORC-232/236 mirrors (#55, #57–#63) are ENGINE as stated because they are the same `EdgeLocator`/navigation the command edge runs, and a violation's failure mode is silent nil rather than a crash.

**HABIT rules** (a violating bundle loads and runs identically):
- **#6 `delivery:` phase/agent_step vocabulary** (chain.ex:732–756) — confirmed: no module outside `lib/catapult/dsl/` reads `Tier.delivery`; the only other `SystemStatus.kinds` reader is `Dsl.Workflow`.
- **#7 navigation edges excluded from readiness walks** — `Edge.navigation` is unread at runtime; the check encodes v5 §4.3's design intent.

Rules with no reader that are LOADER-ONLY rather than HABIT because they mirror a unknown-field/registry discipline rather than a design opinion: #1, #9, #10, #11, #12, #35, #65, #66, #68, `duplicate_names`, `scope_problems`, `enforcement`, `ticket.<source>`, fragment vocabulary (2c/2d), and the `-> tier` landing typing on `self` walks. Two of those hide runtime gaps worth naming: a review tier's `context:` is fully dead (ContextAssembly recomputes from the reviewed tier), and every `ticket.<source>` walk blocks readiness unconditionally.

Load-time gaps surfaced while checking (not rules, but the mirror image): `prompt:` and `draft.grammar` paths are never resolved at load despite §13's claim; `per(X)` scope chains are not cycle-checked but are recursed by `ReadyScopes.tier_drained?`.
